Multi-Grain Flour

Balanced everyday nutrition for the whole family.

Why Multi-Grain?

Most Pakistani households use a single type of wheat flour for everyone. Flour Lab’s Multi-Grain Flour blends multiple whole grains to deliver better fiber, vitamins, and minerals in every roti — without asking your family to change their eating habits.

Key Benefits

  • ✓ Higher nutritional value than plain wheat atta
  • ✓ Extra fiber to support digestion and gut health
  • ✓ Suitable for kids, adults, and elders alike
  • ✓ Great as the “default atta” for daily rotis

How to Use

Use Multi-Grain Flour in all recipes where you currently use normal atta. You can mix it 50/50 with your existing atta during the first week to help everyone adjust to the taste, then move to 100% Multi-Grain once the family is comfortable.

Best For

  • ✓ Families who want a “health upgrade” without big lifestyle changes
  • ✓ Parents who care about long-term health for children
  • ✓ Elders who want easy-to-digest yet nutrient-rich rotis
