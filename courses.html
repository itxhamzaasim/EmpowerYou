Gluten-Free Flour

Perfect for people who cannot tolerate regular wheat or who want light, easy-to-digest meals without feeling heavy or bloated.

  • ✓ Suitable for gluten-sensitive individuals
  • ✓ Supports weight management plans
  • ✓ Versatile for rotis, parathas, wraps & baking
  • ✓ Lighter on stomach than regular wheat
Best for: Gluten intolerance & weight loss Order Gluten-Free

Diabetic-Friendly Flour

A low-glycemic, fiber-rich flour blend designed to support people living with diabetes or pre-diabetes while keeping roti taste familiar.

  • ✓ Supports blood sugar management
  • ✓ Helps you feel full for longer
  • ✓ Ideal for daily chapatis & parathas
  • ✓ Great choice for elders at home
Best for: Diabetes & sugar control Order Diabetic-Friendly

Multi-Grain Flour

A balanced mix of whole grains for everyday family use — designed to replace your regular atta with a richer, more nutritious option.

  • ✓ Higher in vitamins and minerals than plain wheat
  • ✓ Good for digestion due to extra fiber
  • ✓ Suitable for kids, adults, and elders
  • ✓ Ideal as the “everyday family flour”
Best for: Whole-family daily use Order Multi-Grain
