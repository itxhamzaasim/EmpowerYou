High-Iron Women’s Atta

Everyday rotis designed specially for women’s energy and iron needs.

Why Women’s Atta?

Many women in Pakistan silently face iron deficiency, low energy, and weak bones because their daily diet is not tailored to their needs. High-Iron Women’s Atta is Flour Lab’s response — a flour blend focused on supporting better iron and micronutrient intake through the rotis women already eat every day.

Key Benefits

  • ✓ Helps support healthy iron levels as part of a balanced diet
  • ✓ Supports bone health and strength for busy women
  • ✓ Aims to reduce tiredness and low‑energy feelings over time
  • ✓ Suitable for students, working women, mothers, and homemakers

How to Use

Use Women’s Atta for at least one major roti meal a day for the women in the home. You can mix it with Multi-Grain Flour in a 50/50 ratio if you want to serve the same dough to the whole family. Always follow your doctor’s advice if you are on medication or supplements.

Best For

  • ✓ Women with iron deficiency or anemia history (with medical guidance)
  • ✓ Young girls and students with long study days
  • ✓ Working women managing office plus home responsibilities
